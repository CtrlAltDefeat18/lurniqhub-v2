# LurniqHub: Vision and Mission

Status: Draft v0.1, 21/10/2026. Hypotheses to be tested with learners and teachers.

## Vision

Every learner in rural South Africa can see a career in the maritime economy and has a clear, funded route to it.

## Mission

Guide learners from subject choice in Grade 9 to a funded maritime pathway. We do this with accurate pathway information and CAPS-aligned learning content, delivered on the phones and data connections learners already have.

## The problem

- South Africa has a long coastline and active ports, but most rural learners never hear about maritime careers.
- Subject choices made in Grade 9 (Mathematics versus Mathematical Literacy, Physical Sciences) quietly close or open these careers years before learners know it.
- Bursaries, training providers and entry requirements exist but are scattered, change often, and are hard to find on a phone.
- Learners in under-resourced schools often have limited access to Mathematics and Physical Sciences support.

## What LurniqHub is

1. A pathway guide: which subjects to take, what marks programmes ask for, what funding exists, and where to apply. Every entry carries a source link and a "last verified" date.
2. CAPS-aligned learning content, starting with Physical Sciences Grade 11, with maritime examples learners can switch on.
3. A simple progress view so learners and teachers can see what has been covered.

## What LurniqHub is not

- Not a training provider. We do not issue STCW or any other certification.
- Not a replacement for teachers, schools or simulators.
- Not a general-purpose learning platform. Maritime is the focus.

## Principles

- **Accuracy over volume.** A short list of verified opportunities beats a long list of stale ones.
- **Built for the real device.** Pages must work on a low-end Android phone over a patchy 3G or 4G connection.
- **Guidance before gamification.** Points and ranks are secondary to learners reaching a real decision.
- **Minimal data.** We collect only what a feature needs, in line with POPIA, and we are extra careful because most users are minors.
- **Work with schools.** Teachers and school leaders are partners, not an afterthought.

## First pilot

Learners at Toli, early 2027, subject to permission from the school and district and consent from parents or guardians.

## MVP success measures (to be confirmed)

- A learner can register, find the pathway for a maritime career, and name the subjects and marks they need, unaided.
- Learners complete at least one Physical Sciences lesson on their own phone.
- Teachers say the pathway information is accurate and useful.
- Pages load acceptably on a typical learner's phone and data connection.

## Open questions

- Which grades do we serve first: 9 only, or 9 to 12?
- Who owns pathway data upkeep once the pilot ends?
- What do the school and district require before a learner session can take place?
