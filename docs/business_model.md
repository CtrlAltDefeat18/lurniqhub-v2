# Business Model

Status: Draft v0.1, 21/10/2026. Pre-revenue. Everything here is a hypothesis to test. Figures marked [R?] are unknown and must be researched or quoted before use.

## Principle

Learners never pay to access pathway information or learning content. Revenue must come from parties who benefit from better-prepared, better-informed learners.

## Customer segments

| Segment | Who pays | Why they would pay |
|---|---|---|
| Learners | Nobody | Free, always. Data cost is their main cost. |
| Schools and districts | Department, school or donor | Career guidance material, CAPS-aligned content, progress visibility |
| Training providers and employers | Sponsorship or listing fees | Reach qualified, interested learners |
| Funders, CSI and skills bodies | Grants and programme funding | Measured access and outcomes in rural schools |

## Revenue hypotheses (in order of how soon they could be tested)

1. **Grants and programme funding.** Funds the pilot and early build. Not sustainable alone.
2. **School or district licences.** A per-school fee once teacher tools exist. Price [R?], to be set after the Toli pilot.
3. **Sponsored pathways.** Training providers pay to be listed or featured. Guardrail: sponsorship can never change ranking, ordering or accuracy of information. Sponsored entries are labelled.
4. **Programme contracts.** A department or skills body commissions a deployment for a set of schools.

## Value proposition

- For learners: one trusted place to see which subjects, marks and funding lead to a maritime career.
- For schools: curriculum-aligned material and career guidance that costs the school nothing in the pilot.
- For partners: a direct, honest channel to learners who are often never reached.

## Cost structure (approximate categories)

- Hosting and domain: low, using cloud credits and a small server. Amount [R?].
- Content development: the largest cost, mostly founder time. Each course needs subject-expert review.
- Pathway data upkeep: someone must re-verify opportunities on a schedule.
- Learner data costs: consider zero-rating or lightweight pages so data cost does not exclude learners.
- Legal and compliance: POPIA, consent forms, privacy notice. Amount [R?].

## Key risks

- **Grant dependence.** Mitigation: prove school-level value early and test a paid school offer.
- **Pay-to-play erosion of trust.** Mitigation: strict sponsorship rules, published openly.
- **Stale data.** Mitigation: every entry has a source link and a verified date, with a review cycle.
- **Weak evidence of impact.** Mitigation: define simple outcome measures before the pilot, not after.
- **Founder capacity.** Mitigation: keep scope narrow, document everything, automate checks.

## Experiments before spending more build time

1. Show five teachers a mock pathway page. Ask whether they would use it and whether their school would pay anything.
2. Ask two training providers whether they would sponsor a listing and under what conditions.
3. Cost one realistic school deployment end to end, including data and support.

## Stage gates

- **Gate 1 (pilot ready):** permissions secured, MVP working on real phones.
- **Gate 2 (value shown):** learners and teachers use it unprompted and report accurate, useful information.
- **Gate 3 (paying):** at least one party pays or formally commits funding on the basis of the pilot.
