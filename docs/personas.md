# Personas

Status: Draft v0.1, 21/10/2026.

These are composite hypotheses, not research findings. Every field below must be replaced or confirmed with interview data from Toli before the MVP is declared validated. Names are placeholders.

## 1. The subject chooser (primary)

**Placeholder name:** Lwazi, 14, Grade 9
- **Context:** Rural Eastern Cape school. Must choose FET subjects before Grade 10. Has heard nothing about maritime careers.
- **Goals:** Pick subjects that keep options open. Understand what jobs exist and what they pay.
- **Pains:** Limited career guidance. Unsure whether Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy matters. No one at home has worked in this sector.
- **Device and data:** Likely a shared or hand-me-down Android phone, prepaid data, patchy signal.
- **Needs from the MVP:** A plain-language pathway page, subject requirements, and a clear next step.
- **Risks:** Wrong information could shape a real subject choice. Pathway data must be verified and dated.

## 2. The senior learner (primary)

**Placeholder name:** Zinhle, 17, Grade 11 or 12
- **Context:** Already locked into subjects. Weighing marks against programme requirements and funding.
- **Goals:** Know which programmes she qualifies for, which bursaries to apply for, and by when.
- **Pains:** Deadlines are scattered across sites. Marks may be below some thresholds, and she needs to know the alternatives.
- **Device and data:** Own or shared phone, data-conscious.
- **Needs from the MVP:** Marks-versus-requirements guidance, funding list with deadlines, Physical Sciences revision.

## 3. The teacher or Life Orientation educator (secondary)

**Placeholder name:** Mr Dlamini
- **Context:** Teaches large classes with few resources. Responsible for career guidance without much material.
- **Goals:** Reliable, curriculum-aligned material. A way to point learners to real opportunities.
- **Pains:** Little time. Distrusts tools that add admin or contradict CAPS.
- **Needs from the MVP:** Accuracy, CAPS alignment, something he can use in a lesson. A teacher view is deferred until after the first pilot.

## 4. The parent or guardian (secondary)

**Placeholder name:** Ms Mkhize
- **Context:** Decides whether her child may use the platform and share data.
- **Goals:** A safe, legitimate route to a stable income for her child.
- **Pains:** Cost, data usage, and doubts about online claims.
- **Needs from the MVP:** A clear privacy notice, a consent step, and honest information about costs and outcomes.

## 5. The institutional partner (enabler)

**Examples:** School principal, district office, provincial department, training provider, employer, funder
- **Goals:** Verified information, safeguarding of learners, evidence of impact.
- **Needs from the MVP:** Clear data-protection practice, accurate content, and simple reporting once there is something to report.

## Validation plan

1. Interview 5 to 8 learners and 2 to 3 teachers at or near Toli, with permission, before building further guidance features.
2. Record what phone, data and signal they actually use.
3. Ask each learner what they know about maritime careers before showing the platform.
4. Update this file with what we learn and strike any assumptions that turn out wrong.
